Waller ISD Warren Ranch High School

Waller ISD, Warren Ranch High School. My part was character lead on this project. I used the Metahuman Creator, Blender, Photoshop, Substance and Marvelous Designer to create these animations in Ureal Engine.

Warren Ranch High School is a new secondary campus currently under construction for Waller ISD in Hockley, Texas.

Key Details

• Scheduled Opening: Fall 2028

• Location: 16433A Katy Hockley Rd, Hockley, TX 77447 (Harris County)

• Mascot & Colors: The Brahmas, honoring the historical cattle and ranching heritage of the local Warren Ranch (established in 1883).

• Capacity & Scope: Designed to serve up to 3,500 students as Waller ISD transitions away from a single-high-school model.

• Project Cost: Funded via a voter-approved $300 million public bond project.

• Facilities & Programs:

• Comprehensive academic classrooms, dual-credit, and AP options

• Career and Technical Education (CTE) spaces including an agriculture barn, veterinary-style clinic, and cosmetology lab

• Fine arts center, performance theater, and multi-story library

• Athletic complex featuring a stadium, baseball and softball fields, tennis courts, and a field house
