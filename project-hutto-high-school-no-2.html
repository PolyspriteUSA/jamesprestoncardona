Architectural Visualization

Hutto High School No 2

My part in this project was compositing our model over the real world footage of the Hutto High School site. I was also character lead, animation, lighting, and rendering. Compositing our model over the video footage was done in Syntheyes and rendered in Unreal Engine.

• Official Name: Creekside High School

• Mascot: Hippo (retained from district tradition)

• Location: 301 Destiny Ln, Hutto, TX 78634 (current Hutto Ninth Grade Center site)

• Opening Date: Scheduled to open in Fall 2027 (with active construction underway since summer 2025)

• Capacity & Design: Designed to expand up to a 2,400-student capacity, featuring a multi-story main academic building, Career and Technology Education (CTE) spaces, a competition gym, a field house, an auditorium, and a detached fine-arts hall
