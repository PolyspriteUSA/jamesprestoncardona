Architectural Visualization

Liberty Hill High School

This was the first time I used Unreal Engine to composite over video footage. There was quite a bit of learning to do for this project. I used 3DS MAX and Syntheyes to get the footage aligned with the model. Then I imported that to Unreal Engine for a general composite. The final render was exported and composited in After Effects.
