Architectural Visualization

Hanks Middle School

Hanks Middle School, El Paso, Texas. This was my first render for VLK Architects. It was very important to me because I'm from El Paso and this was very close to where I lived. I felt like my education had finally paid off. I focused on good lighting for this project. This project was done in 3DS MAX and VRay. I also used forest pack for the vegetation and people.
