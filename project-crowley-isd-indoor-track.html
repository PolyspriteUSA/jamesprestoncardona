Crowley ISD Indoor Track

This World-class facility is designed to support CISD students’ development. It is home to a rare hydraulic track (just 17 others in the United States) and provides a flexible space for multiple events. The Complex will feature a 200m indoor track and 400m outdoor track, full field events, and seating for 5,000 outdoor and 3,500 indoor spectators.

My part in this project was lead character design. I decided to use the metahumans as the main character generator. It included over 400 custom characters spanning track and field, volleyball, basketball, special events, and a band performance. View more of my work at https://vlkarchitects.com/
